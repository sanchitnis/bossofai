# INTENT — Boss of AI

## One line
**Help people, schools and institutions make an orbit shift with AI: smartly, safely, efficiently, and for the good of the community.**

## The bet
AI makes intelligence cheap. What stays scarce: judgment, taste, ethics, and the nerve to build.
So we don't teach *about* AI. We make people **boss of it**: human decides, AI amplifies.

## Who we serve (and what each one gets)

| Role | Path | The promise |
|---|---|---|
| **Students** | **Srujana Pathway** | Become an **AI engineer** or step into an AI-era career. Build real things, earn proof, not just grades. |
| **Faculty** | **T.R.A.C.K.** | Become a **Superfaculty**: an excellent teacher, researcher and mentor in one. Agents take the drudgery, you keep the guru role. |
| **Institutions** | **T.R.A.C.K. for Institutions** | AI-augmented curriculum, living syllabi, agent-run workflows (accreditation, scheduling, audits). Institutions are also the doorway to reach their students. |
| **Practitioners** | **Reskill / Upskill Tracks** | Mid-career to agent orchestrator. Ship a work-relevant project, not a certificate. |
| **School teachers** | **Teach-the-Teachers** | Confident, safe AI use in class so tomorrow's citizens are ready today. |

## How we teach: projects, not lectures
1. **Micro-challenges**: small, fast, public wins. (Days)
2. **Challenge projects**: real problems from industry, community, research. (Weeks)
3. **Ventures**: the best become products, patents, preprints, startups. (Months)

Rules: learn by doing · open-book, open-agent · every outcome is a **verifiable artifact** (repo, doc, live demo) · human expert always signs off (HEITL) · **mentor support at every stage** · pathways are **customizable** to role, pace, discipline and goal.

## Access: pay it forward
- **Freemium at the start.** Scale first, monetize later.
- **No one is turned away for lack of funds.** Generous scholarships are the default, not the exception.
- **Pay it forward.** Payment can be community contribution, not only money: mentor a peer, teach a school, share a project, review a submission, solve a local problem.
- Revenue (institutional licences, cohorts, grants) exists to fund access, not to gate it.

## Non-negotiables
- **Smartly**: right tool, right task; AI never replaces thinking.
- **Safely**: privacy, honesty, bias, and cheating addressed head-on. Guardrails are part of the curriculum, not a footnote.
- **Efficiently**: use AI and agents to remove drudgery (admin, grading, paperwork) so humans can mentor.
- **Community first**: impact beyond campus (schools, MSMEs, villages, local languages).
- **Authentic**: no fake case studies, no invented stats, every claim traceable.

## The flywheel
Learn → Build → Venture → Serve community → Free up mentoring time → Learn better. **Kaizen** tunes every turn.

## Website intent (bossofai.org)
**Job of the site:** in 10 seconds a visitor knows "this is for me" and takes one next step.

- **Role-first navigation**: Student · Faculty · Institution · Practitioner · Teacher. One landing page each, one clear CTA each.
- **Two reading depths on every page**: *Quick* (punchy, 60 seconds) and *Deep* (frameworks, evidence, citations) for researchers and academics.
- **Tone**: confident, witty, human. Short sentences. No jargon without a reason, no hype without proof.
- **Design**: bold type, high contrast, dark/light, playful motion with restraint, real project artifacts as visuals. Gen Z energy on the surface, scholarly rigor underneath (footnotes, sources, methods are first-class).
- **Proof over promises**: show live projects, outcomes, student/faculty work.
- **Accessible and fast**: mobile-first, multilingual-ready, WCAG AA, light pages.

## What is an "AI Engineer"? (Student target)
Anchored on Andrew Ng's *AI Engineering Skills Map* (DeepLearning.AI), which is built from 10,000+ job postings and expert interviews. Four skills, plus our own fifth:

1. **Build & deploy AI apps**: LLMs, context engineering, RAG, agentic workflows, ML/DL basics, **evals and error analysis**.
2. **Software engineering fundamentals**: design tradeoffs, stack and architecture, data stores, testing.
3. **Work with coding agents**: write clear specs, steer and orchestrate agents, know their limits, avoid risky actions (e.g. touching production data).
4. **Shape the build**: product sense, customer and business context, when to ship a quick MVP vs. slow down.
5. **Boss of AI addition: judgment & responsibility**: safety, ethics, honesty, domain depth. The human signs off.

Careers it opens: AI engineer, agent/workflow engineer, AI product builder, applied researcher, AI-era founder and domain professional.
*Source: Ng's skills map as summarized by [Latent.Space](https://www.latent.space/p/ainews-andrew-ng-gets-into-ai-engineering); verify against his original post before quoting on the site.*

## What is a "Superfaculty"? (Faculty target)
A faculty member who is **excellent at all three at once**: teacher, researcher, mentor. Two markers:
- **Quality (thrive):** better learner outcomes, deeper research, more human mentoring time.
- **Productivity (10X):** agents absorb admin, grading, literature sweeps and prototyping. The 10X is the **target**, and we will measure it (time-on-task before/after) before we claim it anywhere.

## Recognition: how contribution counts
- **Public portfolio** for every learner and faculty member (repos, docs, live demos).
- **Open-source contributions** count.
- **Leaderboard on bossofai.org**, ranked by **peer review + expert-team viva**, not clicks or quiz scores.
- Community contribution (mentoring, teaching a school, reviewing) is how "pay it forward" is recorded and rewarded.

## Evidence policy
- Day one: nothing completed to show, so we show the **process and open invitations**. As work lands, label stage honestly: *idea / prototype / pilot / proven*.
- No outcome numbers, testimonials or partner logos until they exist and can be verified.
- Every metric on the site links to its source or method.
- Gaps are announced as "we are building this", which is a feature, not a flaw.

## Launch content: no showcase yet
No project is complete, so **no project is showcased at launch**. Existing work in `projects/` stays off the public site. Instead the site offers **open invitations**: sample topics people can take on, clearly labelled *"suggested, not yet built"*.

**Sample topics: Students**
- Build a Kannada/English study buddy that quizzes you from your own notes, with evals that prove it is correct.
- Agent that turns a college syllabus into a weekly project plan.
- Red-team a campus chatbot: find 10 ways it fails, fix 3.
- Local problem clinic: an AI tool for a nearby kirana store, farm or clinic.
- Reproduce a recent paper's core result with a coding agent, then audit the agent's mistakes.

**Sample topics: Faculty**
- Redesign one course as project-based, with an AI tutor and open-agent assessments.
- Automate one admin burden (attendance, rubric audit, accreditation evidence) with a human-checked agent workflow.
- Literature-sweep agent for your research area, with source verification.
- Run a mentoring-load dashboard that flags students who need a human conversation.
- Teach a half-day AI workshop at a nearby school.

**Sample topics: Institutions**
- Living-syllabus pilot: one program, quarterly AI-informed updates.
- AI-use policy co-written with students, faculty and staff.
- Agent-run workflow for one department (timetabling, grievance triage, or audit prep).
