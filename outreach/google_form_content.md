## Key Design Choices Explained

> [!TIP]
> **Why only ~8 questions per path?**
> Every additional field reduces completion rate by ~5-10%. A 25-field form will get 30% completion; an 8-field form will get 70%+. You can always ask follow-up questions via email after someone registers.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Why "How many hours/week can you commit?"**
> This is the single most important question for students. It separates casual browsers from real contributors. When you follow up, prioritize people who said 10+ hours/week — they're your beachhead community.

> [!TIP]
> **Why "One sentence: What do you want to get out of PRODIGY?"**
> This open-ended question serves two purposes: (1) it forces the registrant to articulate a concrete goal (not just "I'm interested"), and (2) it gives you data to personalize your follow-up. Generic answers like "I want to learn" signal low commitment.
