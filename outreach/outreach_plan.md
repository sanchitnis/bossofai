## 1. Executive Summary & Strategic Objective

Project PRODIGY (**P**ipeline for **R**esearch **O**riented **D**evelopment with **I**ntelligence of **G**lobal **Y**outh) is a global meta-venture studio empowering global youth and faculty to master human-centric skills (the **4 Cs**: Critical Thinking, Communication, Collaboration, Creativity) alongside frontier AI capabilities.

The objective of this Outreach & Branding Plan is to mobilize key ecosystem stakeholders—university leaders, corporate CTOs, government R&D liaisons, mentors, and global influencers—to collaborate, advise, and incubate downstream ventures.

---

## 2. Branding Narrative & Core Messages

### Primary Tagline & Triad
$$\mathbf{TALENT} \;\;\bullet\;\; \mathbf{ECOSYSTEM} \;\;\bullet\;\; \mathbf{VENTURES}$$
*Follow Your Dreams and Build Our Common Future through Human + Augmented Intelligence.*

### Key Strategic Anchors
1. **The Urgent Crisis**: As AGI automates routine coding and task-based roles, traditional freshers-level IT jobs are vanishing. Educational institutions must assume fundamental responsibility for leading a smooth, empowering societal transition.
2. **The Srujana 4-Stage Pathway**: A flexible developmental framework (*1. Foundational Skills & Readiness $\rightarrow$ 2. Practical Exposure & Internships $\rightarrow$ 3. Product R&D & Deep Inquiry $\rightarrow$ 4. Enterprise Incubation & Diverse Leadership Roles*).
3. **The Co-opetition Model**: Cooperating in pre-competitive research, foundation models, and compute pooling (inspired by India's Amul movement), while competing in market venture creation.

## 5. Phased Campaign Execution Roadmap

- **Phase 1: Ecosystem Foundation & Core Call (Weeks 1–4)**
  - Publish Executive Concept Note and web portal.
  - Launch LinkedIn Thought Leadership series on *"How are we preparing our future citizens?"*.
  - Direct email outreach to inaugural 20 target university hubs and 10 corporate partners.
- **Phase 2: Hub Onboarding & SPOC Activation (Weeks 5–8)**
  - Formalize 1 Executive Sponsor + 4 SPOCs per institutional hub.
  - Onboard inaugural cohort of Tier C (Industry CTO) and Tier D (STEAM+ Domain Expert) mentors.
  - Host national launch Learnathon sprint.
- **Phase 3: Stage 3 R&D & Venture Hatching (Weeks 9–12)**
  - Deploy outcome-based corporate consulting probes.
  - Initiate HEITL expert jury reviews for patentable IP.
  - Convene Global Advisory Board review.
