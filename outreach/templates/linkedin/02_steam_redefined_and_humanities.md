🔬 Why "STEAM" in the AGI Era Must Include DefenseTech, IKS, Psychology & Public Policy

When people hear "STEAM", they often think of standard coding or robotics. But as AI models automate routine technical tasks, the definition of technical & human literacy must expand.

In **Project PRODIGY**, we re-define **STEAM** as a 5-part interdisciplinary spectrum:

- **S (Science & Mathematics)**: Pure/Applied Sciences, Computational Biology, Physics, Cryptography, Graph Theory.
- **T (Technology & Engineering)**: Frontier Systems — **DefenseTech, SpaceTech, Quantum Computing, Optoelectronics, FinTech, AgriTech, HealthTech, DeepTech**.
- **E (Education)**: Andragogy, AI-augmented course design, experiential PBL/CBL.
- **A (Arts & Humanities - Full Spectrum)**: **Psychology, Economics, Sociology, Political Science, Philosophy, Indian Knowledge Systems (IKS), Geopolitics, Public Policy, Architecture, Sports Science, Tourism & Experience Design**.
- **M (Management & Administration)**: Venture building, governance, financial stewardship, cooperative sector economics.

**How are we preparing our future citizens to master these interconnected fields?**

By moving away from siloed lectures and organizing learning around real-world **Role-Based Projects** (Student Sprints & Faculty TRACK Projects).
