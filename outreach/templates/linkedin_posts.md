## 💼 Post 2: Re-defining STEAM — From Rote Coding to Human-Centric Mastery

**Headline**: 🔬 Why "STEAM" in the AGI Era Must Include DefenseTech, IKS, Psychology & Public Policy

When people hear "STEAM", they often think of standard coding or robotics. But as AI models automate routine technical tasks, the definition of technical & human literacy must expand.

In **Project PRODIGY**, we re-define **STEAM** as a 5-part interdisciplinary spectrum:

- **S (Science & Mathematics)**: Pure/Applied Sciences, Computational Biology, Physics, Cryptography, Graph Theory.
- **T (Technology & Engineering)**: Frontier Systems — **DefenseTech, SpaceTech, Quantum Computing, Optoelectronics, FinTech, AgriTech, HealthTech, DeepTech**.
- **E (Education)**: Andragogy, AI-augmented course design, experiential PBL/CBL.
- **A (Arts & Humanities - Full Spectrum)**: **Psychology, Economics, Sociology, Political Science, Philosophy, Indian Knowledge Systems (IKS), Geopolitics, Public Policy, Architecture, Sports Science, Tourism & Experience Design**.
- **M (Management & Administration)**: Venture building, governance, financial stewardship, cooperative sector economics.

**How are we preparing our future citizens to master these interconnected fields?**

By moving away from siloed lectures and organizing learning around real-world **Role-Based Projects** (Student Sprints & Faculty TRACK Projects).

## 💼 Post 3: Call to Corporate CTOs & Industry Leaders — Milestone Consulting & Co-Ops

**Headline**: 🏢 Rethinking Industry-Academia Engagement: Moving Beyond Unpaid Internships

Traditional corporate campus engagement is often limited to annual recruiting drives or passive 2-month summer internships.

In the AI era, this model is broken. 

**Project PRODIGY** introduces 3 structured industry collaboration formats designed for tangible R&D output:

1. **Industry-Mentored Campus Projects (Stage 2)**: Companies submit real problem statements mentored by industry leads physically on campus for academic credits.
2. **Paid Physical Co-Op & Apprenticeship Programs**: Students work on-site at corporate facilities for dedicated weekly schedules, receiving competitive stipends for outcome-driven tasks.
3. **Outcome-Based Corporate Consulting (Stage 3 & 4)**: Industry partners contract specialized student/faculty squads for exploratory R&D probes, paid strictly on milestone delivery.

**How are we preparing our future leaders to deliver enterprise-grade solutions before graduation?**
