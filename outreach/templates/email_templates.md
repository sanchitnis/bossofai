### The Srujana 4-Stage Pathway
PRODIGY embeds a flexible, project-based developmental framework directly into university curricula:
1. **Stage 1: Foundational Skills & Readiness**: AI co-working, self-regulation, and 4 C human skills (Critical Thinking, Communication, Collaboration, Creativity).
2. **Stage 2: Curated Internships & Mentored Projects**: Industry-mentored campus projects and paid physical Co-Op programs.
3. **Stage 3: Product R&D & Deep Inquiry**: Outcome-based corporate consulting, applied research across TRL 1–9, and patentable IP creation.
4. **Stage 4: Enterprising Students & Venture Hatching**: Startup incubation, corporate spinoffs, and diverse leadership roles across public service, defense, social impact, and commercial ventures.

### Institutional Nodal Hub Partnership Structure
Each participating institute establishes a standardized governance node:
- **1 Executive Sponsor**: Vice-Chancellor, Director, or Dean providing institutional support.
- **4 Single Points of Contact (SPOCs)**: 2 Faculty SPOCs (Curriculum & Labs) + 2 Student SPOCs (Clubs & Sprints).
