# Santiniketan: Tagore's School

Rabindranath Tagore founded **Patha Bhavana** in 1901 with five students; it grew into **Visva-Bharati University**. *Source: [Wikipedia: Patha Bhavana, Santiniketan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patha_Bhavana,_Santiniketan), accessed 2026-10-16; supporting description: [Footloose in Me: Shantiniketan](https://footlooseinme.com/shantiniketan/), accessed 2026-10-16.*

## Key ideas (as described by these sources)
- **Closeness to nature** and relationships between teachers and students without artificial barriers; a reaction against rote learning ("cramming like parrots" is Tagore's phrase, quoted on a Gandhi-heritage page).
- **Arts woven into school life:** weekly literary and music evenings where students share their own work; plays and yearly competitions in music, dance and recitation.
- **Student self-governance:** student-run committees (for example environment, health, justice, food, literature) and a magazine produced by senior students.
- **Openness to the world:** professionals invited to discuss current science and social issues.
- **Rural reconstruction** at nearby Sriniketan (from 1921): education tied to village self-reliance.

The teaching methods and curriculum were **not** described in detail in the sources we read. Do not state specifics (such as the exact form of open-air classes) until a stronger source is added.
