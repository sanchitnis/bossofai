# Alpha School: "2 Hour Learning"

A private school network (US) built on a model it calls **2 Hour Learning**: core academics are done in about two hours a day using adaptive software, and the rest of the day goes to life skills, projects, arts and sports. Adults are called **guides** and supervise and motivate rather than lecture. *Source: [Wikipedia: Alpha School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alpha_School), accessed 2026-10-16.*

## What it claims
- Students learn core subjects faster in a short, focused block (promotional claim: roughly twice the progress in two hours).
- The "AI" is described by the founders as adaptive learning software similar to existing tools, not a large language model.
- Freed-up time is used for life skills and projects. The sources we read do not describe the workshop formats in detail.

## Evidence status (read this before using it)
- The speed claims rest on the school's own internal analyses; no independent or peer-reviewed study was found.
- One US state education department described the model as untested when it declined a related application.
- Tuition is high (reported range about USD 10,000 to 75,000 a year), so the model's access and equity story is weak.
- Governance and vendor-interest questions have been reported by the same source.

## Application in PROdiGYM (proposed; Sanjay to confirm)
- **Verdict: reference only, not a design source.** Evidence is too thin to build on.
- Ideas worth keeping as *our own* design choices, not as Alpha's proven results: a short, time-boxed daily block; adults as guides, not lecturers; protected time for applying skills to real life.
- Do not repeat Alpha's performance claims anywhere in PROdiGYM material.
