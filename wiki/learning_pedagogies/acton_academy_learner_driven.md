# Acton Academy: Learner-Driven Studios

A network of mixed-age "studios" (ages about 4 to 18) in which learners drive their own learning and adults act as **guides** who ask questions instead of giving answers. *Source: [Acton Academy: The Acton Model](https://actonacademy.org/model/), accessed 2026-10-16.*

## Key mechanisms (as the school describes them)
- **Socratic guides:** adults facilitate; daily discussions build thinking and clear speaking and writing.
- **Learner ownership:** learners set goals, track progress and work at their own pace; they advance on demonstrated mastery, not on the calendar.
- **Badges** for academic and character skills; **public exhibitions** at the end of each sprint of about four to six weeks; hands-on project "quests".
- **Character as a core aim:** the page names grit, courage, integrity and servant leadership.
- **Community practices:** a written *Contract of Promises*, **Running Partners** and **Squads** for mutual accountability, Servant Leader badges.
- **Hero framing:** learners are treated as young heroes finding a calling. (The model's page does not use the phrase "hero's journey" itself.)

## Evidence status
We reviewed the school's own description only. No independent outcome study has been reviewed. Treat it as a design inspiration, not proof.

## Application in PROdiGYM (proposed; Sanjay to confirm)
- **Verdict: use selectively.** It matches our peer-accountability needs in the first release.
  - **Accountability partners / small squads** and a short written **personal contract** (fits the habit plan, see [[atomic_habits_behavior_design|behaviour design]]).
  - **Badges** for habits *and* character strengths (for example, showing up for a partner).
  - **Sprint and showcase rhythm** (four to six weeks, then a short public-or-private "exhibition" of what you built).
  - **Socratic coaching** style for the coach prompts (ask before telling).
- Not used: age-mixed studios, or the school-governance model (outside our scope).
