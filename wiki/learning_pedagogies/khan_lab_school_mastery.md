# Khan Lab School (KLS)

A US independent school (Palo Alto and Mountain View, California) founded as a laboratory for educational innovation, inspired by Khan Academy. *Source: [KLS: Get to Know Us](https://khanlabschool.org/Get-to-Know-Us), accessed 2026-10-16.*

## What the overview says
- Founding principles include **mastery-based learning** (students move forward when they show mastery rather than by fixed grade levels or schedules) and **student agency**.
- It began with about 30 students in one mixed-age classroom and now runs a lower school and a middle/upper school on separate campuses.
- Knowledge is meant to be **applied in real-world contexts**.

## Limits of what we know
The overview page did **not** describe how independence levels, personalisation, or project and workshop time work. Do not attribute those details to KLS in our documents until a better source is read and added here.
