# AI Driver's License Program
## Redesigning Teaching, Learning, Assessment and Curriculum for the AI Era

### Boss of AI — Academic Platform

---

# Program Theme

### Building Human Capability, Enterprising Ability, AI Fluency and Responsible Human–AI Collaboration

The purpose of AI integration is not merely to improve productivity or automate educational processes. Its purpose is to amplify human capability, strengthen enterprising behaviour, and advance the Human Advantage Framework, enabling learners to create value for themselves, their communities and society.

---

# Expected Program Deliverable

At the completion of the program, every faculty member will possess:

- A fully redesigned AI-ready course in their domain.
- AI-ready Course Outcomes and assessment strategies.
- Redesigned learning activities and assignments aligned to Human Advantage attributes.
- A global AI education benchmarking study.
- A comparative review of AI policies.
- A Human Advantage mapping for the course.
- A department-specific Educate-to-Enterprise implementation roadmap.

These deliverables collectively create a pathway from faculty development to institutional transformation.

---

# Program Objectives

The certification program will enable faculty members to:

- Understand the transformational impact of AI on higher education, society and future careers.
- Develop AI literacy, evaluation skills and responsible AI practices.
- Redesign learning experiences that strengthen human capability and enterprising behaviour.
- Create AI-ready curricula aligned with the Educate-to-Enterprise philosophy.
- Design assessments that verify authentic learning and human contribution.
- Benchmark global AI education practices and policies.
- Develop implementation strategies for strengthening human capabilities through AI-enabled education.

---

# Course Outcomes (CO)

Upon successful completion of the certification program, faculty will be able to:

## CO-1: Conduct AI Course Evaluation as per ADDIE

Evaluate existing courses and assessments to identify AI vulnerabilities, opportunities and redesign priorities using institutional Course evaluation frameworks.

## CO-2: Demonstrate AI Literacy and Responsible Use

Apply AI tools, prompting techniques, orchestration methods, verification approaches and responsible AI practices while addressing issues of bias, privacy, transparency and academic integrity.

## CO-3: Design Authentic and Lovable Learning Experiences

Construct learner-centred learning experiences that strengthen agency, originality, authentic engagement, local relevance and enterprising behaviour.

## CO-4: Develop AI-Resilient Assessment Systems

Design and implement assessment systems that validate authentic learning, process understanding, human contribution and demonstrated competencies.

## CO-5: Design Learning Experiences for Human Advantage

Design learning experiences that intentionally develop Human Advantage capabilities through effective human–AI partnership.

## CO-6: Develop AI-Ready Curriculum and Institutional Implementation Strategies

Design curriculum maps, benchmarking studies, policy reviews and implementation plans that align teaching and learning with the Educate-to-Enterprise philosophy.

# Appendix A
# Human Advantage Framework (HAF)

The Human Advantage Framework defines the capabilities that education must intentionally cultivate in every learner and across every discipline.

1. Commitment and Self-Management
2. Conscientiousness
3. Character and Universal Human Values
4. Citizenship and Making a Difference
5. Curiosity and Scientific Mindset
6. Competency: Domain Knowledge, Skills and Modern Tool Usage
7. Communication, Self-Expression and Media Literacy
8. Collaboration and Teamwork
9. Computational Thinking and Digital Literacy
10. Critical Data-Centric Thinking
11. Complex Problem Solving and Decision Making
12. Creativity and Innovation
13. Community-Based Lifelong Learning
14. Cultural Literacy
15. Courage to Lead and be an Entrepreneur

# Appendix B Institutional Context

## Institutional Vision

> “To become a technologically advanced, sustainable global educational environment dedicated to the wellbeing of all.”

## Institutional Mission

- Provide learner-centric education leveraged with cutting-edge technologies.
- Foster stewardship by nurturing talent, leadership qualities, and entrepreneurial thinking in a safe and secure environment.
- Promote liberal studies and the pursuit of performing arts, literature, sports and other creative and intellectual disciplines.
- Promote a culture of collaboration and cooperation.
- Serve humanity and promote sustainability through higher education based on universal values.

## Core Values

- Ethics and Ownership
- Involvement and Commitment

## Core Philosophy

### Educate to Enterprise (E2E)

**Educate to make students enterprising.**

The purpose of education is not merely to prepare students for employment. It is to develop individuals who can identify opportunities, solve meaningful problems, create value for society, continuously learn, take initiative, demonstrate leadership, and thrive in an AI-enabled world.

## Guiding Principle for the program

> The purpose of AI integration is to strengthen human capability, nurture enterprising behaviour, cultivate character and values, and empower learners to create meaningful impact in society through responsible human–AI collaboration.
