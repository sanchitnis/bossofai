Welcome to the **CSU101** course on Artificial Intelligence. This course focuses on the foundations of AI, Machine Learning algorithms, and their real-world applications.

## Course Objectives
- Understand the core concepts of Neural Networks.
- Explore Deep Learning architectures.
- Implement AI-First curriculum paradigms.
