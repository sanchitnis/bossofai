# Intent: Boss of AI — Establish the Organization

## 1. Problem / Opportunity Statement

India and the world face a critical preparedness gap: educational institutions, the academic community, and society at large are entering an era of Abundant Intelligence without the conceptual frameworks, skills, or institutional structures to navigate it wisely. Most AI-readiness initiatives focus on tools or technical skills, not on building the *cognitive, ethical, and collaborative capacities* needed to work alongside AI as genuine partners.

Boss of AI is the response: a purpose-built organization that functions as a **cognitive workout gym and execution environment** — pairing human experts with autonomous AI agents to produce knowledge, train leaders, and incubate projects that move society from AI anxiety to AI authority.

## 2. Target Audience & Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Role |
|---|---|
| **Education institutions** | Primary beneficiaries — schools, colleges, universities seeking AI-readiness frameworks |
| **Academic community** | Faculty, researchers, and students seeking structured AI capability development |
| **Society / Civil society** | Policy advocates, NGOs, community leaders navigating AI's social impact |
| **Domain experts** | Pedagogy, AI, policy, and ethics professionals who contribute knowledge and validate work |
| **Funders & partners** | CSR programs, government agencies, philanthropies, and impact investors |
| **AI agents (Antigravity)** | Execution partners — operating the AI-Native Project Engine alongside human collaborators |

## 3. Definition of "Done"

The organization is established when:
- [ ] **Legal & governance**: Boss of AI is formally incorporated with a clear governance structure.
- [ ] **Knowledge base seeded**: `wiki/` is populated with foundational methodologies (SPOT-PROBE, Srujana Pathway, Learning Pedagogies) covering all 5 Cognitive Memory tiers.
- [ ] **First 3 projects launched**: Project 1 (AI-Gurukul), and at least two further project tracks, are at Stage 2 (Spec) or beyond.
- [ ] **Collaborative infrastructure**: A working collaboration model is demonstrated — human experts + AI agents producing high-quality deliverables across the 6-stage lifecycle.
- [ ] **First external stakeholder engagement**: At least one institutional partner, funder, or expert committee member is formally engaged.

## 4. Constraints & Non-Negotiables
- **Human-in-the-Loop Gate (HEITL)**: AI agents supply velocity and drafting; human domain experts supply direction, moral grounding, and approval at every gate. No autonomous deployment without human sign-off.
- **Spec-First Discipline**: No draft (`3_draft.md`) produced without a locked spec (`2_spec.md`) for high-stakes organizational documents.
- **Sequential Lifecycle**: All project artifacts are committed in 0→6 sequence; no stage may be skipped.
- **Non-goals**: This project does not define product pricing, hire staff, or select vendors. Those follow once the organization's charter and first projects are established.
