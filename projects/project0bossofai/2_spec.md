# Specification: Boss of AI — Organization Setup

> **Status**: Draft spec — awaiting expert review and lock by Sanjay before Stage 3 drafting begins.

---

## 1. Document Architecture & Outline

The organizational setup deliverable (`3_draft.md`) shall cover:

1. **Organizational Charter**: Mission, vision, values, and the Dual-Engine Architecture (wiki + projects).
2. **Governance Model**: Decision-making structure, HEITL gates, expert committee roles, and accountability.
3. **Methodology Framework**: SPOT-PROBE, Srujana Pathway, and Cognitive Memory Architecture — how they integrate.
4. **Founding Projects Portfolio**: Description of the first 3 active projects and their lifecycle status.
5. **Partnership & Funding Strategy**: Institutional partnerships, government/CSR engagement model, and resource plan.
6. **Operational Roadmap**: Milestones for the first 12 months of operations.

## 2. Voice, Tone & Reading Level
- **Tone**: Strategic, visionary, evidence-grounded, and action-oriented.
- **Reading Level**: Institutional leader, funder, and domain expert.
- **Length**: 8–12 pages for the full organizational charter deliverable.

## 3. Explicit Non-Goals
- We are NOT drafting legal incorporation documents or articles of association.
- We are NOT producing a financial model or detailed budget breakdown.
- We are NOT selecting specific technology vendors or platforms.
- We are NOT designing the internal HR or staffing structure at this stage.
