# Specification: AI-Gurukul 2.0 — Center of Excellence Design

> **Status**: Draft spec — awaiting expert review and lock by Sanjay + Expert Reviewers before Stage 3 drafting begins.

> **Gate requirement**: `1_intent.md` must be validated by at least one pedagogy expert and one AI/domain expert before this spec is locked.

## 1. Document Architecture & Outline

The Stage 3 draft (`3_draft.md`) shall be a full **Program Design Document** covering:

1. **Executive Summary**: The AI-Gurukul 2.0 model — the problem, the solution, and the ask — in 2 pages.
2. **Pedagogical Foundation**: The 7 sources of inspiration; synthesis principles; why this model vs. alternatives.
3. **Program Architecture**: The 4-level skill ladder; mastery-gating logic; progression criteria; viva structure.
4. **Curriculum Design**: Level-by-level learning objectives, project types, peer evaluation rubrics, and assessment criteria (by learner track: school teachers, college faculty, institutional leaders).
5. **Integrity & Quality Assurance System**: Expert Committee charter; evaluation-calibration methodology; anti-gaming safeguards.
6. **Campus Design Principles**: Physical and virtual environment; Santiniketan-inspired studio model; infrastructure requirements.
7. **Business Model & Sustainability**: Revenue streams; pricing logic; corporate cross-subsidy design; CSR/government engagement strategy.
8. **Roadmap & Milestones**: Phase 1 (Months 0–6), Phase 2 (Months 6–18), Phase 3 (Months 18–36); success metrics.
9. **Impact Measurement Framework**: North Star Metric; leading indicators; classroom deployment measurement methodology.
10. **The Ask**: Seed capital estimate; institutional partner MOU template; Expert Committee recruitment criteria.

## 2. Voice, Tone & Reading Level
- **Tone**: Rigorous, evidence-grounded, inspiring. Serious ambition with proof of pedagogy.
- **Reading Level**: Funder, institutional leader, and pedagogy specialist.
- **Length**: 25–35 pages for the full Program Design Document.
- **Formatting**: Each section prefaced with a 2-line "key question this section answers."

## 3. Explicit Non-Goals
- We are NOT writing a detailed financial model with precise revenue projections.
- We are NOT selecting specific technology vendors, AI tools, or platforms.
- We are NOT drafting legal agreements, MOU templates, or campus lease agreements.
- We are NOT designing the detailed staffing org chart or job descriptions.
- We are NOT committing to a specific campus address or partner school names.

## 4. Open Questions Before Locking

1. **Curriculum depth**: How much technical AI content (model internals, fine-tuning, RAG) belongs in Levels 1–2 vs. being reserved for Level 3+?
2. **Viva panel composition**: Should the Expert Committee for Level 1 vivas be the same individuals as for Level 3–4, or should there be tiered panels?
3. **Evaluation rubric source**: Do we adapt Minerva's Habits of Mind rubric directly, or design a new AI-specific rubric grounded in it?
4. **Campus model for Phase 1**: Co-working/rented space first, then dedicated campus — or dedicated from day one? What are the program integrity implications?
5. **Government partnership strategy**: Which specific ministry or state-level body (NEP 2020 implementation cell, NCTE, state education departments) is the primary engagement target?
6. **Open-source platform**: Which specific open-source AI-for-education projects should Level 3 participants contribute to? Or do we build our own?
