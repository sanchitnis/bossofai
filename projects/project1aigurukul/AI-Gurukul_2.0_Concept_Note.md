# AI-GURUKUL 2.0

## A Center of Excellence for AI-Powered Education

### Concept Note & Pitch Narrative — Bangalore, India

---

## Slide 1 — The Hook: One Question

> **What if every teacher in India could harness AI — not fear it?**

India has 9.5 million teachers. Almost none have been trained to use the most transformative technology of their generation. Not because they don't want to — but because nobody has built a place where they can *learn by doing*.

We are building that place.

## Slide 2 — The Problem: The Missing Middle

**Education is about to be rewired by AI. The people holding the wire were never shown how.**

- **Teachers are anxious, not empowered.** Generative AI arrived in classrooms before any training did. Most educators either avoid it or use it superficially.
- **Traditional teacher-training is broken.** Lecture-based, certificate-chasing, one-time workshops — with zero stickiness. Most workshop knowledge fades within 30 days without practice.
- **Colleges lack faculty who can *teach* AI.** Industry pays 3–5× academic salaries. Institutions can't hire their way out — they must **train their way out**.
- **Students suffer the compounding effect.** A teacher uncomfortable with AI produces students unprepared for an AI-first workforce.

**The gap isn't information. The gap is a learning *environment*.**

**No single model is imported wholesale. Each contributes one proven principle, and the synthesis is designed for India's educators.**

## Slide 4 — The Solution: AI-Gurukul 2.0

**A center in Bangalore where school and college faculty train on AI — through mastery, projects, peer evaluation, and work that matters.**

- **No lectures. No professors at the front.** Only projects, peers, and practice.
- **Mastery-gated progression:** complete one level to unlock the next. No shortcuts.
- **AI tutors personalize every learner's path** — so a humanities teacher and a computer science professor can both start from where they are.
- **Work must be socially useful** — every project solves a real problem in a real classroom, school, or community (Gandhian SUPW principle).
- **A campus with trees, not corridors:** learning happens in studios, workshops, and open spaces (the Santiniketan way).
- **The deepest insight: you can't teach AI-enabled pedagogy with lecture slides. You have to *experience* it.**

## Slide 5 — The Four Skill Levels: How Learning Happens

Progression is strictly sequential. Each level ends with a **Viva** before the next unlocks.

### 🌱 Level 1 — AI Literate *(1-week residential intensive, on campus)*

**Foundation in AI thinking, tools, and ethics.**

- Learn via AI-tutored personalized paths + Socratic studio discussions
- Core: how LLMs work, prompting, AI for daily teaching tasks (lesson plans, quizzes, feedback), bias & ethics, student privacy
- **Project (SUPW):** build one working AI-assisted tool for your own classroom and deploy it in a real lesson
- **Peer evaluation:** each participant's project is rigorously evaluated by two peers using a structured rubric (Minerva Habits of Mind)
- **Viva:** a 45-minute defense before an **Expert Committee** of AI specialists, senior educators, and industry practitioners — defending not just the project, but the *quality of the peer evaluations you gave* (Slide 6)

### 🌿 Level 2 — AI Practitioner *(1-week residential intensive, on campus)*

**From user to designer of AI-enabled pedagogy.**

- Design AI-native lesson sequences, assessments, and personalized learning paths
- Data-informed teaching: reading learner analytics, iterating on real outcomes
- **Project (SUPW):** run a full AI-augmented unit (2–3 weeks) in your institution with your own students; bring back real data
- **Peer evaluation + Viva** (same rigor as Level 1)

### 🌳 Level 3 — AI Innovator *(virtual, open-source collaborative project)*

**Building in public, for everyone.**

- Fully virtual, global cohort
- Participants contribute to **open-source AI-for-education projects** (e.g., open tutoring tools, assessment frameworks, low-resource-language AI resources) — collaborative, version-controlled, public
- SUPW at scale: the community is the beneficiary
- Peer review happens through the open-source process itself (code review, documentation review) — calibrated by the Expert Committee
- **Viva:** public project defense + review of the candidate's open-source contributions and review quality

### 🏆 Level 4 — AI Mentor *(virtual, open-source collaborative project)*

**Training the trainers.**

- Participants now *run* the peer-evaluation system for Levels 1–2, facilitate Socratic studios, and steward open-source projects
- Must shepherd at least two learners through a full level and one open-source module to completion
- **Viva:** assessed on mentee outcomes, community contribution, and evaluation calibration — judged by the Expert Committee

## Slide 6 — The Viva & Peer-Evaluation Integrity System

**Peer evaluation is the engine of learning here — but only if it stays honest. Our safeguard: the Expert Committee.**

```javascript
  Learner builds project
        │
        ▼
  Evaluated by 2 peers (structured rubric — Habits of Mind)
        │
        ▼
  EXPERT COMMITTEE VIVA (45 min)
    ├─ Defend your project and your reasoning
    ├─ Show how you used the peer feedback
    └─ THE TWIST: committee audits YOUR peer evaluations
       of others — depth, criticality, calibration
        │
        ├── Evaluation too soft / "helping"? → Learner must
        │   re-evaluate; flagged for calibration training
        └── Passed? → Level unlocked; evaluator quality
            score feeds your own progression record
```

**Why this matters:** In pure peer systems, evaluation can drift into mutual back-scratching. By making the *quality of your critiques of others* a graded, examined part of your own journey — reviewed by experts who themselves model excellent evaluation — we turn peer review into a **discipline of critical thinking**, not a courtesy.
