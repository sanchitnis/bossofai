# Getting Started with Boss of AI (bossofai.org)

## Quick Start in 3 Steps:

1. **Review Strategic Direction:** Read `README.md` and `strategy/concept-note.md` to align on the vision of Cascading Transformation and Abundant Intelligence.
2. **Understand the Pathways:** Read `wiki/srujana_pathway.md` (for learners) and `wiki/track_framework.md` (for faculty).
3. **Launch or Join an AI-Native Project:** Open this workspace in your agentic IDE. Go to the `projects/` directory, create a new project based on `_template-project/`, and instruct your AI agent to help you populate `0_context.md` and `1_intent.md`.
